# MAIN ENTITY: Faculty of Engineering & Technology
**URL:** https://gokuluniversity.ac.in/College/Engineering

## About Faculty of Engineering
The Faculty of Engineering (FoE) at Gokul Global University (GGU) promotes excellence in engineering education, advanced research, and innovation through a rigorous academic environment. Its research-informed curriculum integrates core engineering sciences with emerging technologies, ensuring strong theoretical foundations and professional competence. Teaching–learning practices include expert lectures, thematic workshops, industry immersion, and inquiry-driven projects that develop critical thinking, advanced problem-solving, and leadership capabilities. Hansaba College of Engineering and Technology (HCET), a key constituent of FoE, offers AICTE-approved Diploma, Undergraduate, and Postgraduate engineering programs. The institution is supported by modern infrastructure comprising specialized laboratories, a research-oriented library, seminar halls, and academic support systems aligned with contemporary quality standards. Diploma programs focus on essential technical skills, undergraduate programs emphasize experiential learning and employability, and postgraduate studies promote specialization and research engagement. Doctoral programs prioritize original research and high-impact scholarly contributions. Hansaba Institute of Technology (HIT) offers future-ready undergraduate engineering programs with a strong emphasis on Computer Science–driven domains, intelligent systems, data-centric technologies, connected devices, and AI-enabled electronics, aligned with current and emerging industry needs. HCET houses the Aero Vision Laboratory, established in collaboration with AVPL International, featuring advanced drone technologies for hands-on learning and research in unmanned aerial systems. Additionally, HCET is recognized as an Anchor Institute for Robotics and Artificial Intelligence under Scheme-1 of the Centre for Entrepreneurship Development, Government of Gujarat, serving as a hub for innovation, skilling, and capacity building in frontier technologies. The Faculty of Engineering welcomes aspiring engineers to engage in a dynamic academic ecosystem dedicated to innovation, excellence, and future-focused technological advancement.

## Vision
To be a world class technical institution with significant international impact and strong local commitment.

## Mission
To offer best training and guidance to students by recognizing their inherent potential to help them meet their ambitions in the field of engineering and technology.

## About
Diploma Engineering Electrical
Electrical Engineering is a field of engineering that generally deals with the study and application of electricity, electronics and electromagnetism. Electrical engineering, an amalgamation of what is now called electrical, electronics and communication, instrumentation, computer engineering etc. is the largest of all engineering disciplines.Four well recognised branches of electrical engineering are power, communications, electronics and control systems. In a broader sense, this field covers a range of sub-disciplines including those that deal with power, optoelectronics, digital electronics, analogue electronics, artificial intelligence, control systems, electronics, signal processing and telecommunications. Overlapping of this field with computer has opened up the door to a career in almost any industry.

## About
Diploma Engineering Mechnical
Mechanical Engineering, the broadest of all Engineering disciplines deals with design and production of tools, machines and all other mechanical equipment to be used in industries. It can be said as the back bone of industrial development. Mechanical engineering is concerned with all types of machinery in industries and all aspects of their mechanism and functioning; the design, development, construction, production, installation, operation and maintenance; such as large steam and gas turbines, components of thermal power stations, internal combustion engines, jet engines, machine tools, air conditioning and heating machines, refrigerators to name a few. They not only design and create new products, but also develop materials for them and ways of making them.
